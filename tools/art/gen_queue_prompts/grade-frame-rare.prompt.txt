An empty ornate vertical card frame for a dark gothic fantasy action game's level-up cards, 2:3 portrait, front view, symmetrical. ONLY the border exists: a band about 8 percent of the width all around; the whole inner window and everything outside the border are flat magenta so they can be cut out. Semi-realistic painterly metalwork with a bright rim light along the right and top edges, deep dark planes, readable as a shape at small size. Grade: rare. Tarnished brass band with pointed lancet-arch ornaments at the four corners and thin inlaid lines of pale gold #e0c47e around the band. Richer than the common frame, only a faint warm glow.
