An empty ornate vertical card frame for a dark gothic fantasy action game's level-up cards, 2:3 portrait, front view, symmetrical. ONLY the border exists: a band about 8 percent of the width all around; the whole inner window and everything outside the border are flat magenta so they can be cut out. Semi-realistic painterly metalwork with a bright rim light along the right and top edges, deep dark planes, readable as a shape at small size. Grade: jackpot, the most precious. Blackened-silver band with a rich amber-gold #efac59 inlay and thin gaps of glowing amber light along the inner edge; a crown of small sharp points at the top centre and short rays of light leaking outward at the corners. Clearly more precious than the rare frame yet built the same way. No gems, no figure.
