Full-length character concept art of one adult demoness, an ancient being who lends her power in bargains. Semi-realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, high finish, muted ambient light, one accent colour used sparingly. Vertical 2:3 portrait, the whole figure visible from the top of her horns to the tips of her boots, about 90 percent of the image height, nothing cropped. She reads as a woman, not a creature: natural human-toned skin, human eyes with pupils and luminous irises, flawless and ageless, horns fused with a crown, short dark claws on the fingertips only. Her clothing is fully opaque: a sculpted bodice and hip piece in thick structured fabric, long gown or long skirt, boots; no sheer cloth, no nudity, nothing more revealing than the previous version. BACKGROUND: a flat, plain, very dark near-black charcoal (#121316) with only a faint soft vignette, no architecture, no scenery, so the figure can be cut out easily; only what she sits on stays in the picture, and a thin dark floor line under it. Storm demoness, the youngest-looking of her kind, late twenties, short angular jaw, sharp eyes with raised outer corners, slim springy build, deep navy hair lifted by static, horns that split like lightning with a crown. Pose: perched sitting on the top of a large cracked bronze bell lying half-buried in rubble, one boot up on the bell, the bell and her boots fully visible, a single thread of lightning between two fingers of one raised hand, looking down with a sharp lopsided smile, one corner of the mouth higher. Accent colour periwinkle #b9caff.
