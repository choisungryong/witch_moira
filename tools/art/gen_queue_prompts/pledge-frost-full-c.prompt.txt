Full-length character concept art of one glamorous ADULT demoness in her thirties, an alluring and playful dealer of power and luck who toys with the one she serves. Semi-realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, high finish, strong rim light, muted dark palette with ONE accent colour used sparingly. Vertical 2:3 portrait. She STANDS (no throne, no seat, no crown, no tiara): the whole figure is visible from the top of her horns to the soles of her boots, about 90 percent of the image height, nothing cropped, both feet on the ground. She reads as a woman, not a creature: natural human-toned skin, human eyes with pupils, flawless; short dark claws on the fingertips only. She holds NOTHING out to the viewer and carries no object in her raised hand; one hand is raised to shoulder height with the palm down and the fingers loose, as if about to set a spinning reel turning. The look is confident, teasing and tempting about power and luck only: no sexual posing, no suggestive pose, no reaching toward the viewer. Her clothing is fully opaque: thick, structured bodice and hip piece, long skirt or long coat, boots, nothing more revealing than a closed bodice with a moderate neckline and bare forearms; no sheer cloth over the body, no nudity. BACKGROUND: a flat, plain, very dark near-black charcoal (#121316) with a faint vignette, no architecture, no scenery, no ground effects, so the figure can be cut out easily; only a thin dark floor line under her feet. Her face must be a distinct individual face, not a copy of anyone else. Frost's side. Face: a round chin, thick dark brows, a clearly shaped mouth with a faint cold smile, pale natural skin tone (NOT blue or grey skin), luminous pale-cyan irises, looking straight at the viewer. Hair: deep blue-green fading to white frozen tips, gathered high on the head with part of it left hanging, no tiara (NOT blue-black, NOT silver-grey, not worn loose to the waist). Horns: one pair of long ice horns sweeping back from the temples, not joined to any crown. Outfit: flowing layered gown, a fully opaque fitted underdress in dark teal with silver ornaments and thin translucent icy outer cloth drifting over the skirt only; one small mended patch of different cloth stitched at the cuff of one sleeve, the only sign of an ordinary life. The cold stays on her hands and hem: a little frost on the hem, snow falling straight down; no vertical bars or grid-like frost. Her free hand gathers up the skirt. Walking-stand pose, one foot slightly ahead. Accent colour icy cyan #9ce7ef. Variation: a longer, narrower silhouette in a long coat-like gown with a high collar of ice crystals; the horns are shorter and more curved.
