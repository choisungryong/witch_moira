Full-length character concept art of one glamorous ADULT demoness in her thirties, an alluring and playful dealer of power and luck who toys with the one she serves. Semi-realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, high finish, strong rim light, muted dark palette with ONE accent colour used sparingly. Vertical 2:3 portrait. She STANDS (no throne, no seat, no crown, no tiara): the whole figure is visible from the top of her horns to the soles of her boots, about 90 percent of the image height, nothing cropped, both feet on the ground. She reads as a woman, not a creature: natural human-toned skin, human eyes with pupils, flawless; short dark claws on the fingertips only. She holds NOTHING out to the viewer and carries no object in her raised hand; one hand is raised to shoulder height with the palm down and the fingers loose, as if about to set a spinning reel turning. The look is confident, teasing and tempting about power and luck only: no sexual posing, no suggestive pose, no reaching toward the viewer. Her clothing is fully opaque: thick, structured bodice and hip piece, long skirt or long coat, boots, nothing more revealing than a closed bodice with a moderate neckline and bare forearms; no sheer cloth over the body, no nudity. BACKGROUND: a flat, plain, very dark near-black charcoal (#121316) with a faint vignette, no architecture, no scenery, no ground effects, so the figure can be cut out easily; only a thin dark floor line under her feet. Her face must be a distinct individual face, not a copy of anyone else. Fire's side. Face: heart-shaped with a pointed chin, feline almond eyes with dark brown irises, straight dark brows, a wide confident mouth with a knowing curl. Hair: glossy jet black, worn in a long high ponytail with a few ember-orange strands, one shorter side-swept lock (NOT wine red, NOT chestnut brown). Horns: a short pair of smooth curved black horns that stand free, with no crown or band joining them. Outfit: close-fitting dress and short cropped jacket in charcoal black and burnt orange, thigh-length boots; a small worn leather herb pouch hangs from her belt at the hip, the only sign of an ordinary life. Embers drift in the air but no fire touches or circles her feet and nothing burns upright around her. Stands tall with her weight on one leg, free hand on the hip.
