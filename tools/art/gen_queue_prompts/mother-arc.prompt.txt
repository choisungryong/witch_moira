Full-length concept art of one adult woman in her early thirties, standing, vertical 2:3 portrait. Semi-realistic painterly style with a high finish, rich but restrained colour, a strong rim light, dark ruined background fading to near-black. The whole figure is visible from head to feet, about 85 percent of the image height, with empty space above the head; her face is in the top quarter. The upper half of her face, from the brow to the eyes, lies in deep shadow cast by her own hair or a hood edge; her mouth and chin are visible, lips closed, an expression that is stern and tired, never smiling. She looks at the ground to one side, never at the viewer. Her clothing is modest: sleeves or cloak cover the arms or end at the elbow, the skirt reaches below the knee, all fabric over chest and hips is thick and opaque, flat low-heeled working shoes. No crown, no bell, no child's object, no weapon. Her power marks are part of her body and are few. Storm's mother, who gave up her voice. Platinum hair in a long braid; grey eyes in shadow. A fern-like branching lightning scar winds around her throat. A short pair of thin cracked horns; lightning patterns on both forearms. One hand is raised to chest height, caught in a gesture, two fingers of it resting on the back of the other hand. Her mouth is closed. Around her the raindrops hang still in the air and a bolt of lightning in the dark sky is frozen mid-strike, a silent scene. Background: collapsed cloister, accent colour periwinkle #b9caff.
