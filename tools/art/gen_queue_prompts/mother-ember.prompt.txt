Full-length concept art of one adult woman in her early thirties, standing, vertical 2:3 portrait. Semi-realistic painterly style with a high finish, rich but restrained colour, a strong rim light, dark ruined background fading to near-black. The whole figure is visible from head to feet, about 85 percent of the image height, with empty space above the head; her face is in the top quarter.
