Full-length character concept art of one adult demoness, an ancient being who lends her power in bargains. Semi-realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, high finish, muted ambient light, one accent colour used sparingly. Vertical 2:3 portrait, the whole figure visible from the top of her horns to the tips of her boots, about 90 percent of the image height, nothing cropped. She reads as a woman, not a creature: natural human-toned skin, human eyes with pupils and luminous irises, flawless and ageless, horns fused with a crown, short dark claws on the fingertips only. Her clothing is fully opaque: a sculpted bodice and hip piece in thick structured fabric, long gown or long skirt, boots; no sheer cloth, no nudity, nothing more revealing than the previous version. BACKGROUND: a flat, plain, very dark near-black charcoal (#121316) with only a faint soft vignette, no architecture, no scenery, so the figure can be cut out easily; only what she sits on stays in the picture, and a thin dark floor line under it. Frost demoness, a cold exact figure of her mid-thirties: long narrow face, high cheekbones, tall and thin, face almost still, lips closed, pale natural skin (NOT blue or grey), human eyes with luminous pale-cyan irises, very long loose blue-black hair falling below the waist (NOT white or silver), two backward-curving translucent ice horns and an ice crown fused with them, decorations of silver and crystal. The blue cold stays at her fingertips and does not spread up the arm. Accent colour icy cyan #9ce7ef. Pose: seated, composed: sitting upright on a frost-covered stone throne with an ice-crystal back, hands folded in her lap, one small ice shard resting on her fingertips; gown of midnight blue and silver with long sleeves.
