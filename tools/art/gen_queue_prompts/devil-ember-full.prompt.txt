Full-length character concept art of one adult demoness, an ancient being who lends her power in bargains. Semi-realistic painterly dark-fantasy style, high finish, muted ambient light, one accent colour used sparingly. Vertical 2:3 portrait, the whole figure visible from the top of her horns to the tips of her boots, about 90 percent of the image height, nothing cropped. She reads as a woman, not a creature: natural human-toned skin, human eyes with pupils and luminous irises, flawless and ageless, horns fused with a crown, short dark claws on the fingertips only. Her clothing is fully opaque: a sculpted bodice and hip piece in thick structured fabric, long gown or long skirt, boots; no sheer cloth, no nudity, nothing more revealing than the previous version. BACKGROUND: a flat, plain, very dark near-black charcoal (#121316) with only a faint soft vignette, no architecture, no scenery, so the figure can be cut out easily; only what she sits on stays in the picture, and a thin dark floor line under it. Fire demoness, the heaviest and most mature of her kind, early forties, broad jaw, warm bronze skin, long loose wavy deep wine-red hair, large upward obsidian horns with a blackened-iron crown set with a few embers. Her arms are ordinary human skin from shoulder to knuckles (no soot, no cracks). Oxblood and blackened-gold gown with a tall flared collar. Pose: leaning back at ease on a throne of charred stone and iron, legs crossed, the whole throne and her boots visible, one elbow on the armrest, the other hand holding a single small glowing coal between two fingertips, looking down at the viewer, an unhurried, generous, amused smile. Accent colour molten orange #ff943d.
